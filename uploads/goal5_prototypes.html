🥔 What counts as 1 serve?
Potato (cooked)½ cup = 1 serve
Sweet potato (cooked)½ cup = 1 serve
Corn (whole kernel)½ cup = 1 serve
Taro / Yam (cooked)½ cup = 1 serve
Weight per serve ~75g
Key rule: ½ cup cooked = 1 serve always
